Good to know

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers about the Grand Dine demo experience.

Can I make a real reservation?

No. The reservation form saves a local demo request in your browser only. It does not contact a restaurant.

Can I pay online?

No real payments are processed. Payment choices are visual demo options only.

Will my cart stay after refreshing?

Yes, the cart is stored in your browser's localStorage on that device and browser.

How does the promo code work?

Use GRAND10 in the cart to apply a sample 10% discount, capped at ₹500.

Can I use this on mobile?

Yes, the layout adapts to mobile, tablet, and desktop screens.

Are customer accounts secure?

The profile page is only a local demo and is not a secure authentication system. Do not store sensitive information.
